DraftKings and FanDuel, side by side, the moment a line moves

Line moves
Waiting for the first move. Lines are quiet midweek; Sunday mornings and live games move constantly.

Best price is the higher odds on the same number. When the two books post different numbers, for example −3 −110 against −3.5 +100, both are shown and neither is called best: a half point is worth money and the board will not pretend otherwise.

A price flashes when it moves and keeps its old value beside it for a few seconds. Each book shows how fresh its data is; a book that stops answering turns stale and its prices grey out while the other keeps updating. How it works and what was measured
